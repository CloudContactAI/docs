In the CloudContactAI ecosystem, Clients provide a Tenant mechanism enabling you to segregate phone numbers and contacts. 

The Client mechanism enables a group of users to share common access with specific privileges to phone numbers, contacts, SMS messages, Emails, and voice communication. 

You can access your active Client by navigating to the upper right hand corner of the CloudContactAI user interface and clicking the blue "Client: (Client Name)" button. See images below for reference. 

<Image width="100%" src="https://files.readme.io/e7639df-CloudContactAI_SS_2_resize.png" />

You can create multiple clients within your account, and access them from the Client button in the upper right hand corner as well as in the settings page.
