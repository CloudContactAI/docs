WHICH COUNTRIES DO YOU OFFER LOCAL PHONE NUMBERS FOR?\
We currently offer local phone numbers for the below countries.

Australia\
Austria\
Belgium\
Canada\
Chile\
Czech Republic\
Denmark\
Finland\
France\
Germany\
Guatemala\
Hungary\
Ireland\
Israel\
Italy\
Mexico\
Netherlands\
Norway\
Poland\
Portugal\
Singapore\
Spain\
Sweden\
Switzerland\
United Kingdom\
United States of America\
If you plan on using a local phone number from one of the countries above, you should notify your customers in that country of your local phone number. Customers in that country are more likely to reply to a local number.

For example, if you buy a Germany number, you'll need to notify your German customers of your German number by placing it on your German website. Messages to German numbers will automatically be from your German number. Also, German customers are more likely to reply to a German number.

🚧\
Ping Wigdet

The Ping Widget does not include international numbers.

Adding a Local Phone Number\
In CCAI, you can add a local phone number for a specific country.

Click on the Menu bar and go to **Settings**\
Click on **Number Settings**\
Click on **Add New Country**\
In the drop-down field, select the country of where you want the local phone number from\
Enter a preferred area code for that country\
Click on **Add Preference**
