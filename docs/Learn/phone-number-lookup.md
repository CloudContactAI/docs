Sending texts to the wrong person (a reassigned number) or a known litigator can result in TCPA lawsuits costing between $500-$1,500 per message.  Our AI systems will automatically scrub any contact details the moment they are uploaded and run the phone number by reassigned, litigious, and carrier databases.  &#x20;

<br />

## What are we looking for?

###

### Recently Reassigned Numbers

Check numbers against the Reassigned Phone Number Database before sending. The last thing we want to happen is for you to reach out to someone only to find a new contact who hasn't given consent to your communication.

- Confirms current phone number ownership
- Lookup valid for 60 days (reduces API costs)
- Adjustable grace period per account
- Prevents TCPA violations from stale contact data

### Litigious Phone Numbers

To protect our customers in debt collection and marketing, we also search for and highlight potentially volatile contacts who have had a potential history with lawsuits.

- Tracks numbers involved in TCPA lawsuits
- Flags law offices and debt settlement companies
- Auto-adds flagged numbers to Do-Not-Call list
- Protects you from serial litigators
