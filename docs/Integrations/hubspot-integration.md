## Integration

Integration only becomes accessible after the user has upgraded from a trial account when the settings tab is available.  To integrate to HubSpot, this option will be found under the appropriately named "integration" tab.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/66eda2815d8432e8c27cd5e24516930e3f6897880a46c54cc36537122b8cb016-install_hubspot_app.png" />

Clicking on the button to install the CCAI app will take the user to a login and HubSpot account selection.  Permissions must be open to allow the app to install.  CCAI will make a request to have access to your HubSpot contacts and data

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/63d89687d3f8a0be6295f31dc411b7a60d2ded0e9a27af383d4d457eaa43fba8-install_hubspot_account_select.png" />

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/75ab5d08cb91e6f7278449ca32c40f8812e387e7146aad84a6109d8f629925b6-install_hubspot_request.png" />

After connecting to the CloudContactAI account,, the "integrations" tab will update with the HubSpot account credentials.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/39207e6c1682fadd244890a478bd6f9fb5971847847dd48c1f69d875c49d0fde-app_installed.png" />

## Synchronization

Contacts on the HubSpot account will not be automatically ported to the CloudContactAI records, but they can be imported just as easily as uploading a CSV file of contacts.  On the upload page is a radio button to upload from HubSpot.  They can be either uploaded individually or from entire lists already arranged on HubSpot's records.
