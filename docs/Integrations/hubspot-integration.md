## Synchronization

Contacts on the HubSpot account will not be automatically ported to the CloudContactAI records, but they can be imported just as easily as uploading a CSV file of contacts.  On the upload page is a radio button to upload from HubSpot.  They can be either uploaded individually or from entire lists already arranged on HubSpot's records.
