1. Login to CloudContactAI
2. Navigate to SMS Campaigns
3. Click on the AI Agents tab
4. Click on the Create AI Agent button
5. ![](https://files.readme.io/d54fdb585052fa22b90f7af7cce07a90d32292a83ec7abc867d3f582b836accc-image.png)

   Enter the name of your Agent and context that should be passed into the prompt. Please contact [support@cloudcontactai.com](mailto:support@cloudcontactai.com) if you'd like to upload your own knowledge base to FAISS.
6. ![](https://files.readme.io/7c74e56bffe914178dc037978da09fb8957348f118da31a5e6ad783041a8263e-image.png)

   Click the Save button.
7. Navigate to Settings
8. Click on the General Settings tab
9. Select Enable Bot Conversation
10. Select Bot Should Answer As Custom Agent
11. Specify the name of your Agent. 
12. ![](https://files.readme.io/8f8cbe66448e4f52a3bca1ec850611b96f5ebbadd95979827abc25cf058a821f-image.png)

    Hit the Save Button
13. Text your inbound phone number. Engage in a conversation with you Agent.
